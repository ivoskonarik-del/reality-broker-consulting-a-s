Luxury Smart Home for Sale – 4-Bedroom Bungalow, 268 m² – Úherce (Plzeň-North), Východní 182

We are pleased to offer for sale a luxuriously equipped and spacious single-storey family home with a smart home system, featuring a layout of 4 rooms + kitchenette and a total usable area of 268 m². The property is located in a quiet residential part of Úherce (Plzeň-North district).
